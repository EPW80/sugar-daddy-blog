What to ask before bloodwork

Take a short list of questions to your appointment and leave with a plan for the results.

By Sugar Daddy Editorial

Editorial draft · Publication date pending

A lab order can be a row of acronyms by the time you see it. The person ordering it can tell you what each test is for and how the results will guide the next conversation. Bring a short list so you can leave with those answers.

What are we looking for?

Ask what each test checks and whether you’re looking at a new question or following an existing result. If something comes back outside the reference range, ask what else your clinician considers before deciding what it means for you.

How should I prepare?

Ask whether the specific test requires fasting or other preparation. Bring a list of medicines and supplements and ask for instructions about what to take that day. MedlinePlus recommends asking your care team about preparation and telling them if you were unable to follow the instructions.

Who will follow up, and when?

Before you leave, ask how the results will arrive, when to expect them, and whom to contact if you have questions. If you usually see numbers in the portal before anyone calls, ask how to schedule time to go through them.
