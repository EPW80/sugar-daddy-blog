Training on a busy week

Put training on the calendar and give yourself a shorter version for crowded days.

By Sugar Daddy Editorial

Editorial draft · Publication date pending

Look at the week you actually have. Pick two time slots with the fewest competing obligations and write them down. A Tuesday lunch break may be easier to protect than a Thursday evening that already belongs to three other people.

Decide what you’ll do before you show up

Write down the first few moves from a routine you already know. Get your clothes and shoes in one place the night before. If you’re walking, pick the route before you step outside.

Give each slot a shorter version

On a crowded day, use the time that’s available. Take the shorter loop or do a short session of familiar movements. Federal physical activity guidelines count bouts of moderate or vigorous aerobic activity of any duration toward the weekly total.

Make next week easier

Write down what you did and what got in the way. If you never make the evening slot, move it. A useful training plan accounts for traffic, dinner, and the odd meeting that runs an hour over.
