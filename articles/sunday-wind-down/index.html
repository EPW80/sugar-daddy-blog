The Sunday wind-down

Give Monday a quieter start with a repeatable Sunday evening routine.

By Sugar Daddy Editorial

Editorial draft · Publication date pending

Sunday gets noisy right around the time the house gets quiet. You remember the meeting, the laundry, the thing you said you’d do Friday. Give that list somewhere to go before you get into bed.

Close out the week on paper

Spend five minutes making a short list for Monday. Pick the first thing you’ll do and leave the rest in the notebook. Lay out whatever you’ll reach for in the morning, then put the list away.

Give the last hour a recognizable shape

Pick a quiet activity you’re happy to repeat. Read a few pages, take a shower, or put on a record. The National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute recommends keeping a regular sleep schedule and using the hour before bed for quiet time. Start with the part that fits your evening and repeat it on a weeknight.

Give the phone a place to land

Pick a charging spot beyond arm’s reach. If you need the phone for calls, keep the ringer available and put the screen out of sight. The idea is to make the next choice easy when you’re tired.
