Editorial standards and corrections

Sugar Daddy Editorial

Sugar Daddy’s editorial desk covers training, sleep, and everyday health decisions. The desk researches, drafts, and edits stories published under its shared byline.

Sources and review

We link to the sources behind our factual claims. We ask a qualified reviewer to check medical guidance before publication and name the reviewer, their credentials, and the review date on the article.

Corrections

To flag an error, reply to an Inner Circle letter with the article link, the passage in question, and any supporting source. When a correction changes the substance of a story, we add a dated note to the article explaining what changed.
